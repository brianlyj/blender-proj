MARI DANCE + EMOTES - NUMPAD CONTROLS
============================

Open Classroom-emotes.blend in Blender 5.2. The Mari Emote Numpad Controls
addon is installed on this computer. Move the mouse over the 3D viewport
and turn Num Lock on.

+  2 Phut Hon - fluid      Refined 10-second dance with weight shift and follow-through
-  2 Phut Hon - original   The preserved original 10-second dance bone animation

1  Crossed-arm sass        Half-lidded eyes, folded arms, weight shift
2  Happy bounce            Closed happy eyes, smile, bouncing fist pumps
3  Cheerful twirl          Full turn, raised hands and follow-through
4  Deal with it            Pixel sunglasses and a raised finger
5  Cheeky wink             One-eye wink, sparkle, toothy grin, crossed arms
6  Paw dance               Alternating raised hands and shoulder rhythm
7  Shy hand clasp          Hands near the chest, blush, head tilt
8  Double point            Both index fingers point forward with a sway
9  Dejected bow            Drooping head/ears, relaxed arms, gloom marks
0  Relaxed idle            Stop and return to the resting pose

Press the same key again to return to idle.
Numpad Enter pauses/resumes; from idle it replays the previous emote.
Numpad decimal (.) enables/disables the emote shortcuts so you can use
Blender's normal numpad view controls. These shortcuts apply only to the
3D viewport in an emote scene; other projects retain normal controls.
The N sidebar has an Emotes tab with the same controls as buttons.

Emotes are six-second loops at 24 fps; both dances are ten seconds.
Switches ease from the current pose over eight frames (about 0.3 seconds).
The temporary blend is replaced with the full action before the next loop.
Revised movements use staggered hand gestures, anticipation, softer knees,
body counter-motion, and delayed hair/ear/bag movement. The full-body rig,
facial controls and effects travel together when switching actions.
The original Classroom-dance.blend and its video are unchanged.

ON ANOTHER COMPUTER
Install mari_emote_controls.py with Blender's Add-ons > Install from Disk,
enable Mari Emote Numpad Controls, then open Classroom-emotes.blend.
The same controller is embedded in the blend's Text Editor for inspection.
The scene does not require enabling automatic execution of embedded scripts.

EDITING
Refined actions are named EMOTE 0 through EMOTE 9 with a fluid suffix.
Dance actions are DANCE | 2 Phut Hon - fluid and DANCE | 2 Phut Hon | ORIGINAL.
Select the Mari armature and
use the Action Editor to edit them. Expression control properties are
keyed on the armature alongside the bones. The Emotes collection holds
head-skinned smile lines, mouth shapes, sunglasses and expression effects.
The build and validation scripts are under classroom/scripts/.
